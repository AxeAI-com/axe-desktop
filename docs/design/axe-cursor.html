Axe cursor

Three directions for the pointer Do it shows while it works in other apps. Each is shown over a light and a dark window, with the action word it carries while it works.

A. Glass

Frosted white pill, app icon badge, blue wordmark. Calm and native, but it fades into white pages, which is most of what Do it works over.

B. Brand blue

Solid #060ae6 pill with the white mark, a soft #3438ff glow and a hairline ring. Reads on white and on black alike, and nobody would mistake it for anything but Axe.

C. Ink

Near black glass with the app icon badge and a blue action word. Sharp on light windows, but on dark apps only the glow separates it from the window.

Pick: B, brand blue

It is the only one that holds contrast over both light and dark windows without leaning on a shadow, so it stays legible in a demo video whatever app is behind it. The blue pill with the white mark is the Axe app icon stretched into a label, so it is unmistakably the brand. The glow does the separating, the hairline ring keeps the edge crisp at 2x, and the action word sits behind a thin divider instead of a dot.
